# Readora — AI-Powered Reading Companion

## Product Vision

**Read more. Remember more.**

Readora is a premium AI-powered reading companion. It is not just a book tracker. The book tracker is the foundation for a larger system that helps users discover books, track reading, capture ideas, understand what they read, connect knowledge across books, and get personalized recommendations.

### Core Product Loop

**Discover → Track → Read → Capture → Understand → Remember → Discover Next**

### Product Layers

1. **Library** — books, TBR, shelves, statuses
2. **Reading** — progress, timer, goals, streaks
3. **Notes & Highlights** — capture personal knowledge
4. **AI** — explain, quiz, flashcards, compare, apply
5. **Knowledge** — concepts, connections, personal reading history
6. **Discover** — personalized recommendations and mood-based discovery
7. **Analytics** — reading insights and reports
8. **Monetization** — freemium + premium subscription

---

# 1. Product Positioning

Do not position Readora as:

> Another Book Tracker

Position it as:

> **Your AI Reading Companion**

The product should answer:

- What am I reading?
- How far am I?
- What should I read next?
- What am I learning?
- How can I understand this better?
- What have I learned across all my books?

### Differentiation

Existing competitors are strong in tracking, statistics, reading timers, social/community features, and recommendations.

Readora should focus on:

> **Understanding and remembering what you read.**

The key differentiators are:

- AI Book Companion
- Personal Knowledge
- Cross-book connections
- Ask My Library
- Personalized recommendations with explanations
- AI-powered reading insights
- Offline-first reading tracking
